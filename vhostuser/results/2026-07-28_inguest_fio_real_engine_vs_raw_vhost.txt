Attribution (consistent with P-1.5(i), measured pre-vhost at the
engine level: p50 5 us, max 175-420 s):
- The transport is EXONERATED: 99.95% of writes complete <= 139 us
  through the full vhost-user path incl. the engine fast path, and the
  burst average (27.3k) approaches the raw transport ceiling (35k).
- The stalls are the engine's synchronous backpressure drain
  (awaitBackpressure: pendingBytes > 256 MiB high watermark drives
  DrainToBackendCtx inline; random 4 KiB writes scatter dirty blocks
  across chunks, making the drain slow). One write pays the entire
  drain: minutes-long guest-visible I/O freeze. Reads are steady (3.1k
  IOPS = backend/cache bound, no stalls) -- read leverage remains
  cache/backend, as recorded in F7 qualifier 2.
- Same-boot raw-file numbers rose from the frozen 28.5k (commit
  a2fc287) to 35.4k after demoting per-request Info logs to Debug --
  the logging cost ~24% and was measured into the earlier numbers
  (single runs, shared host; treat the delta as indicative).

Implications recorded in spinifex DECISIONS.md F7 / STATUS:
