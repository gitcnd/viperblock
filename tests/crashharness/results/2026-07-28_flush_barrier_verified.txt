P1.4 slice 1 verification: SyncOnFlush barrier, end-to-end via nbdkit
=====================================================================
Date: 2026-07-28. Plugin built from feat/replicated-wal-durability
(SyncOnFlush enabled in the plugin), served via nbdkit over unix socket
against the predastore 3-node loopback cluster; strace -f -tt on the
serving process, qemu-io as the NBD client.

BEFORE (2026-07-27, 2026-07-27_flush_no_fsync_finding.txt): 50 explicit
NBD flushes -> ZERO fsync-family syscalls.

AFTER:
- 1 write + 50 flushes: fsyncs only where data was dirty (first
  barrier), later empty barriers correctly skip the fsync -- a barrier
  with nothing new to persist need not sync.
- 20 interleaved write+flush pairs: 24 fsyncs total = 20 barrier
  fsyncs + open/close-path syncs (raw trace:
  2026-07-28_flush_barrier_verified.strace). qemu-io confirmed all 20
  writes acknowledged.

Guest fsync on the served path is now power-loss durable up to the
local disk's guarantees. Remaining for full P1.4: the volatile-cache
FUSE loss-count rig (verifies the fix under simulated power loss), and
extending the barrier over F2 peer replication when the transport
lands.
