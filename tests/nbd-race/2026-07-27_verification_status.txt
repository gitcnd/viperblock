NBD close/open lifecycle race: verification status (honest record)
===================================================================
Date: 2026-07-27/28 overnight. Fix under test: connectionLifecycleMutex
(commit 6a871d2).

Measured:
- POST-fix plugin: 60 write(4M)+reconnect iterations, 0 failures.
- PRE-fix plugin (correctly built with GOFIPS140=v1.0.0 after the first
  attempt crashed on the fipsboot guard): 30 identical iterations,
  0 race hits.

What this means, stated plainly:
- The cheap reproducer (4 MiB write then reconnect) does NOT trigger
  the race even without the fix: its close-side drain window is tens of
  milliseconds. The one wild occurrence (2026-07-27, during P-1.5
  benching) followed a ~400 MiB write burst, whose close-side drain to
  predastore runs for SECONDS -- a much wider window.
- Therefore the fix is NOT empirically validated by differential
  reproduction. It stands on: (a) the mechanism being code-evident
  (Close ends in RemoveLocalFiles; nothing serialises the next Open
  against it; the wild failure's ENOENT matches exactly), (b) fix-by-
  construction (a single mutex ordering Open after teardown), and
  (c) no regression post-fix (60 clean iterations + the plugin builds
  and serves normally).
- A faithful reproducer needs a long close-side drain (e.g. 100k-write
  burst then immediate reconnect, ~5 min/iteration). Queued as a
  nice-to-have; the fix itself is low-risk regardless.

Also banked: GOFIPS140=v1.0.0 is mandatory when building anything that
embeds viperblock (fipsboot init panics at runtime otherwise).

FINAL (2026-07-28 02:54): long-drain reproducer (100k-write burst per
