3. REMAINING MECHANISM (named for slice 4; P1.6 stays [~]):
   A writer blocked at the 256 MiB watermark cannot be released until
   the in-flight drain's FLUSH PHASE completes, because pendingBytes
   only decrements per uploaded chunk (createChunkFile) and
   DrainToBackendCtx is strictly flush-everything THEN assemble/upload.
   With up to 256 MiB to flush, the first freed chunk trails the block
   point by seconds -- the observed 16.3 s worst case. Fix shape:
   pipeline chunk assembly/upload with the batched flush (consume
   flushed batches into chunks immediately), or an equivalent
   incremental hand-off in WriteWALToChunk. That is engine-drain
   redesign work, deliberately not attempted in this session's
   timebox.
