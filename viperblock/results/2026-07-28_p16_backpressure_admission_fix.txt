3. RESIDUAL STALL ROOT-CAUSED (discriminating probe, temporary test,
   numbers preserved here + journal; probe deleted after run):
   Fill 256 MiB buffered (65,536 records), concurrent 4 KiB writer
   sampling every 5 ms, then direct Flush():
     flush duration          724.7 ms
     concurrent WriteAt max  721.5 ms   (pre-flush max 49 us)
   => flushLocked holds Writes.mu for the ENTIRE flush; every write
   admitted past backpressure stalls behind it in WriteAtCtx. At
   in-guest scale (multi-GiB WAL file, 200 ms WAL-syncer fsyncs, chunk
   assembly running concurrently) this phase stretches to the observed
   ~35 s. The admission fix cannot bound this; it is a separate
   mechanism.

REMAINING for P1.6 (the only remaining content of the gate): bound the
flush lock-hold -- flush in bounded batches releasing Writes.mu between
them (and/or move per-record WAL appends off the lock), plus WAL
segment rotation so syncer fsyncs stay bounded; then re-run this rig
and set the final numeric bound (100 ms-class target) with the human.
